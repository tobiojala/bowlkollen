# Bowlkollen Product Constitution v1.0

## Purpose

Bowlkollen is **not** another bowling statistics website.

BITS already owns official competition data.

Svenskalag already manages clubs.

Bowlat is building a comprehensive statistics platform.

Bowlkollen exists for something different.

**Bowlkollen is the home of a bowler's entire bowling life.**

Everything we build should strengthen that mission.

---

## Product Vision

Our goal is to become the app every Swedish bowler opens before, during and after every bowling activity.

A bowler should never need to think:

*"Where did I save that?"*

Everything should already exist inside Bowlkollen.

Not because we have the most features.

Because everything works together.

---

## The Four Pillars

### 1. Prepare

Help bowlers prepare before bowling.

Examples:

* Upcoming matches
* Team lineups
* Competition schedules
* Bowling center information
* Oil patterns
* Ball recommendations
* Personal notes
* Captain messages
* Availability
* Travel planning

---

### 2. Play

Help bowlers during bowling.

Examples:

* Live scores
* Live match stories
* Lane assignments
* Match statistics
* Follow teammates
* Tournament mode
* Notifications
* Live commentary

---

### 3. Improve

Help bowlers become better.

Examples:

* Personal statistics
* Ball performance
* Spare percentages
* BK Rating
* Saved games
* AI insights
* Practice tracking
* Equipment history

---

### 4. Remember

Every bowling season tells a story.

Examples:

* Season Atlas
* Match memories
* Tournament history
* Personal milestones
* Team achievements
* Photos
* Notes
* Auto-generated stories
* Career timeline

---

## Product Principles

Every feature should satisfy at least one of these principles.

✓ Makes bowling easier.

✓ Makes bowling more social.

✓ Helps someone improve.

✓ Helps someone remember.

✓ Saves time.

✓ Creates delight.

If a feature satisfies none of these, it probably should not exist.

---

## Design Philosophy

Clean.

Fast.

Personal.

Mobile-first.

The interface should feel alive without being noisy.

Every screen should answer:

"What matters to me right now?"

Never overwhelm users with statistics.

Show the right information at the right moment.

---

## What Bowlkollen Is NOT

We are not trying to become:

* A copy of BITS.
* A better Svenskalag.
* A statistics clone.
* A generic social media platform.
* A feature collection.

Every feature must support one unified experience.

---

## Development Principles

Prefer depth over breadth.

One amazing feature is better than five average ones.

Every feature should connect to existing systems.

Avoid isolated tools.

Avoid dead-end pages.

Avoid duplicate functionality.

Everything should strengthen the ecosystem.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Imagine opening Bowlkollen five years from now.

It already knows:

* Who you bowl with.
* What equipment works.
* Which centers you love.
* Your personal milestones.
* Your captain.
* Your next competition.
* Your team's journey.
* Your bowling memories.

Bowlkollen becomes your bowling companion throughout your entire career.

That—not another statistics page—is what we are building.

---

## Standing Instruction for AI Collaborators (Claude, Gemini, etc.)

When proposing a feature, first explain how it supports this Constitution.

If it doesn't clearly support the vision, challenge the feature instead of implementing it.

This turns the AI from a code generator into a product partner.
